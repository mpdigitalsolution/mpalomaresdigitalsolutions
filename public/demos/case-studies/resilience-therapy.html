Phase 1 & 2: Telemetry Audit & Full-Stack GTM Rebuild

Before launching campaigns, an audit revealed that the primary conversion goal (appointment booking page load) had recorded zero recent conversions. Without accurate conversion signals, Google's smart bidding algorithms bid blindly. We deployed an enterprise Google Tag Manager container with dual Google Ads and GA4 event streams.
