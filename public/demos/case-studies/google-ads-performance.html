Marlon's 4-Step Search & Telemetry Framework

Whether operating under the strict non-profit guidelines of Google Ad Grants or bidding on competitive clinical healthcare keywords, every turnaround follows this standardized, scientific execution playbook.

Step 01

Telemetry & Goal Audit

Inspect conversion actions for inactive status, tracking loops, and zero-conversion leaks before spending ad dollars.

Step 02

Full GTM Instrumentation

Deploy Google Tag Manager with Conversion Linker, calls from website, form submissions, and dual GA4 event parameters.

Step 03

Negative Conflict Pruning

Audit search term reports to eliminate cannibalizing negatives, purge low-intent single-word queries, and secure 8/10 Quality Scores.

Step 04

Smart Bidding Scaling

Transition to Maximize Conversions bidding once clean conversion data flows, locking in low CPC and compliant interaction rates.
