The Turnaround Blueprint

1. Conflicting Negative Keyword Removal

Identified account-level negative keywords that inadvertently blocked high-intent commercial terms related to the non-profit's mission. Unblocking these queries restored qualified search impressions immediately.

2. Pruning 27 Redundant Keywords

Eliminated 27 low-quality, overlapping search terms that were cannibalizing budget and depressing Quality Scores. Consolidated impressions into high-relevance exact and phrase match ad groups.

3. Ad Grants Compliance Protection

Maintained strict alignment with Google Ad Grants guidelines: ensuring no single-word keywords, maintaining Quality Scores ≥ 3, and elevating interaction rate to 14.00% (far exceeding the 5.0% suspension threshold).
