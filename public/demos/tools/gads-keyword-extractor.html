🚨 The Unfiltered Problem

Direct exports from Google Keyword Planner contain massive volumes of noisy data that will waste budget or trigger Ad Grants account suspensions:

  • × Out-of-Budget CPCs: Bids ranging up to $26.63+ (incompatible with Ad Grants $2.00 cap).
  • × Single-Word Terms: Banned under Google Ad Grants policy (triggers account suspension).
  • × Noisy 30+ Columns: Difficult to isolate commercial opportunities quickly.

📊 Input Metrics Parsed

The script dynamically indexes headers: Keyword, Avg. Monthly Searches, Competition, Top of page bid (low range), Top of page bid (high range), and 12-month historical monthly volumes.

Google Ad Grants Strict Compliance

Google Ad Grants requires non-profits to stay above a 5.00% account-wide CTR floor, prohibits single-word keywords, and caps bids at $2.00 unless smart bidding is activated. This tool codifies those requirements into programmatic rules, eliminating human error before campaigns launch.

Automated Healthcare Policy Scanner

Google Ads strictly penalizes uncertified healthcare claims, prescription drug mentions, and clinical guarantees. The Apps Script contains a regex dictionary of policy-flagged medical terms, ensuring every harvested keyword is verified safe before adding to ad groups.

Dynamic Opportunity Scoring Formula

Calculates a normalized score balancing search volume against top-of-page low bids. High-volume terms with low bids receive maximum score (e.g. health of community: 22,200 searches at $1.13 low bid → Opportunity Score 171), allowing immediate prioritization.

Native Spreadsheet Integration

No external API subscriptions or complex third-party setups needed. Team members simply paste Keyword Planner exports into Google Sheets, click the custom Google Ads Keyword Filter → Optimize menu, and receive formatted results in seconds.
